The most asked question

Regular or deep — which one do I need?

Most villas need a regular clean weekly, and a deep clean every few months or between long-stay guests.

Regular cleaning

Your weekly reset

From Rp 150,000

What's included

  • Sweep, mop and vacuum all floors
  • Bathrooms — toilets, showers, sinks, mirrors
  • Kitchen surfaces, sink, stovetop wipe-down
  • Beds made (linen change on request)
  • Empty trash, replace bags
  • Dust visible surfaces

Not included

  • Inside fridge, oven, microwave
  • Heavy lifting or moving furniture
  • Maintenance work
Deep cleaning

The full reset

Rp 300,000–600,000

Everything in regular, plus

  • Inside fridge, oven, microwave, cabinets
  • Behind and under appliances and furniture
  • Tile grout scrubbing & limescale removal
  • Windows inside & out, frames, sills
  • Ceiling fans, light fixtures, AC vents
  • Detailed bathroom & kitchen scrubbing
