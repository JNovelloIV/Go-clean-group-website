## 2. Manifesto — "The idea"

**Eyebrow:**
> The idea

**Body (4 paragraphs):**

> We felt *disconnected* too. From the food we ate. From the hands that grew it. From the land, the seasons, *each other*.

> And it wasn't just a feeling. Monocrops were stripping the soil bare. Poultry came dosed with antibiotics and saline. Produce was grown in chemicals strong enough to kill what pollinates it. *"Fresh"* had started to mean shipped, gassed, and dressed up to look alive. We were eating it too. A slow-motion emergency. We called it normal.

> We wanted somewhere that closed the gap. Where animals fertilise the soil, the soil grows the food, and what we don't eat feeds the animals. *Nothing wasted. Nothing pretending.*

> A place for long tables, open fires, and *people who want to reconnect* — with food, with soil, with each other.

*Notes:* ✅ LOCKED — P1 reworked (confessional opener, past tense, "hands" → sensory + echo to gallery). P2 is new: names the specific industrial-food harms (monocrops, antibiotics/saline in poultry, pollinator-killing chemicals, fake "fresh"). Closes with "A slow-motion emergency. We called it normal." — the boldest moment on the site. P3 unchanged (was P2). P4 = old P3 with the apologetic ending ("We're not ready yet. But we will be.") cut — stripping that line removes the last piece of old-brand voice; with P2 carrying real edge, an apologetic coda would defuse everything.

**Scratchpad note (cross-cutting):** Add a dedicated **Production** section between Locate and Building. Covers: the animals and their roles, market garden, syntropic systems, scale, organic/no-chemicals, freshness (grown here, not shipped from two islands away). Tone target: "I want to be there. I want that life." — not a spec sheet.

**Voice/positioning notes from Google reviews (for Production, testimonials, quotes later):**
- Concrete scope confirmed: "organic permaculture garden, chicken, ducks & fish"
- Visitor language that already matches the brand: *"timeless space where you can truly reconnect with nature"*, *"gardens filled with one beautiful plant after another"*, *"stunning view of the rice fields"*, *"paradisiacal"*, *"feel like home"*
- Existing activities mentioned by visitors: cooking classes, yoga resets, lunches from the garden

### TODO-DESIGN-06 — Dual-channel Subscribe form (email OR WhatsApp) + segmentation chips

**Why:** Many of the audience — especially Indonesians and expats living on Lombok — primarily use WhatsApp, not email. Forcing an email field creates friction for the local audience. International visitors still prefer email. Solution: let the reader pick their preferred channel up front; the rest of the form is identical.

**Where:** Subscribe section. Replaces the previous email-only form.

**FORM STRUCTURE (top to bottom):**

1. **Channel chooser:** "How should we reach you?" with two toggle options: [Email] [WhatsApp]. Visually a segmented control or two pill buttons. One must be selected; default to Email.

2. **Conditional contact field** (changes based on channel):
   - If Email: standard email input, placeholder "you@email.com"
   - If WhatsApp: phone number input with country code selector (defaults to +62 Indonesia, switchable to other codes), placeholder "WhatsApp number"

3. **Q1 chip group** — labeled: "I am... (optional, pick any)"
   - A neighbor on Lombok
   - A chef
   - An expat looking for clean food
   - A traveler curious about the hill
   - Just here for the letters

4. **Q2 chip group** — labeled: "I'm drawn to... (optional, pick any)"
   - Weekly produce, when it's available
   - The long table dinners
   - Sourcing for my kitchen
   - The slow build of a place
   - Getting my hands in the soil

5. **Submit button:** Subscribe

6. **Fine print:** "Free & quiet. Unsubscribe anytime — reply STOP on WhatsApp, or unsubscribe from any email. We won't share your contact — we barely check ours."

**Visual treatment for chips:**
- Pill-shaped, soft outlined, casual. NOT checkboxes. NOT corporate-survey aesthetic.
- Unselected: sage outline, transparent fill, sage label.
- Selected: harvest gold outline, faint gold-tinted fill, gold label.
- Hover: warm shift.
- "(optional, pick any)" subtitle: visible but de-emphasized — sage, smaller type, beneath question label.
- Generous whitespace between Q1 and Q2.

**Visual treatment for channel chooser:**
- Should match the chip aesthetic but read as a *choice between two options* rather than a multi-select. Segmented control or two pills with clear single-selected state.
- The chooser should be smaller / less visually heavy than the contact field below it. It's a setup, not the main act.

**Backend / data:**
- ONE submission contains: contact value (email OR phone number), channel type (email/whatsapp), Q1 selections (array), Q2 selections (array).
- Validation rules differ by channel: email format vs phone number format.
- Channel value should be stored as a tag/property on the subscriber record so future campaigns can route to the right channel.
- WhatsApp signups need explicit opt-in language captured (the act of submitting with WhatsApp selected = consent to receive messages on that channel). Most WhatsApp Business APIs require this for compliance.
- For implementation: a third-party form provider (Formspark, Tally, Fillout) that supports conditional fields and forwards to ESP + WhatsApp tool will be cleanest. Native form-to-ESP integrations rarely handle dual-channel + conditional logic well.

**Don't:** add phone number AND email both, name field, country/location field, company field. The form is: channel chooser + one conditional contact field + two optional chip groups + submit. Nothing else.
